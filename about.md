Hi, I'm Jaysen — a second-year Computer Science student at the **University of Indonesia**
and a member of **NetSOS** (Network, Security & Operating System SIG) at **Ristek CSUI**.

## What I Do

I play CTFs and create challenges. In **COMPFEST 2025**, I served on the Scientific
Committee for the Capture The Flag competition, authoring forensics and web exploitation
challenges — including PCAP analysis, Rust malware reverse engineering, Windows AD1 artifact
recovery, SSRF with DNS rebinding, and a YAML deserialization challenge for the Attack &
Defence finals.

I also delivered an open class on web exploitation and created challenges for
**Pekan RISTEK 2025**.

## This Blog

This is where I share CTF writeups and what I learn along the way. Most posts focus on
the thought process, techniques, and investigation steps rather than just the final
solution.
